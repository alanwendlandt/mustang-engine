Engine reference & model scope

Represents the architecture of a carbureted Ford 289 pushrod V8. Two banks of four cylinders, sixteen valves, one camshaft in the block. Ford lists a 4.000-inch bore and 2.870-inch stroke. Original firing order: 1–5–4–2–6–3–7–8. This static anatomy model does not simulate valve timing or combustion.

References: Ford engine dimensions · Ford engine history. Carburetor and accessory appearance varies by year and engine specification. No Ford affiliation.
